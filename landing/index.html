How it works

You ask the way you normally would. The skill does three things before you see a page.

  1. 01

    Reads your product

    Your repo, docs and notes. Every claim on the page comes from a real source. What it can’t check, it asks you.

  2. 02

    Keeps only what fits

    A section stays only if a fact about your product needs it. Template blocks without one are cut.

  3. 03

    Builds and ships

    One page, checked on desktop and phone, deployed to Vercel. A waitlist too, if your product needs one.

The usual sections have to earn their place.
